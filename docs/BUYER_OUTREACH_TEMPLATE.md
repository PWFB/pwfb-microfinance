## Outreach guardrails

- Do not say the product is profitable, has paying customers, is licensed, or is production-ready unless verified.
- Do not send source code, secrets, personal data, or production access in the first message.
- Do not publish an asking price as an appraisal. The previously discussed US$15,000 negotiable figure is only an exploratory hypothesis.
- Do not claim a message was sent until it has actually been sent and a record exists.
- Prioritize strategic buyers who can reuse the software and independently assess compliance, integrations, and engineering quality.
